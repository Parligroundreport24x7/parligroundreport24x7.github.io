Hamare baare mein

Parligroundreport 24x7 (Parli Ground Report) Parli Vaijnath, jila Beed, Maharashtra se chalne wala digital news platform hai. Hamara focus local khabrein, ground reports aur aam logon ke mudde hain, saath hi Maharashtra, desh aur duniya ki zaroori khabrein bhi.

Hum khabar tak khud pahunchne, tathya jaanchne aur janta ki aawaz ko saaf shabdon mein rakhne par bharosa karte hain.
